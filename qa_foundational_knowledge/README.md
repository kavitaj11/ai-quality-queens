# Kavita A. Jadhav | Testing experience

I’m a Java-certified test automation lead, SDET and founder of K11 Software Solutions. Across more than twelve years in quality engineering, I’ve tested systems where the screen shows only the beginning of a transaction. An accepted quote doesn’t prove that revenue was recorded correctly. An enrollment file can arrive without the policyholder’s balance being updated. I design automation around the downstream evidence that proves the business outcome.

## K11 Software Solutions · AI-assisted quality engineering

In my current work I build automation frameworks with Playwright, Selenium, Serenity BDD, Cucumber and Vibium, alongside API and microservice testing. I explore how generative AI can propose test cases, assist locator maintenance and explain failures. I bring the same question to AI-generated tests that I brought to financial reports: what independent evidence shows the result is correct?

## Verizon · Edge network service assurance

I collaborated with network lab engineers to validate E-Line and edge service workflows. Starting from a controlled lab fault, I traced fault collection, alarm correlation and enrichment through event and ticket creation. I also tested maintenance workflows and used Serenity BDD for automation. Following that sequence helped distinguish a missing alarm from an alarm that arrived but never produced the expected operational action.

## VMware · Subscription commerce

I led end-to-end automation for SaaS and subscription commerce and coordinated an onsite and offshore team of more than twenty. Working with pricing operations and QA, I translated commercial terms into test conditions: direct versus channel sales, prepaid versus recurring SKUs, volume tiers, overage and negotiated discounts. I followed orders from CPQ quotes into downstream finance, combining UI, service and Oracle database checks to verify bookings and revenue.

## E*TRADE Corporate Services · Equity administration

On the Control-M project, I worked on submitting batch jobs through an API, waiting for completion, retrieving reports and comparing their business fields with baselines. A successful job could still produce an incorrect report. The comparator had to ignore expected differences in generated fields without masking changes in business data.

On Equity Edge, I traced a user action to database audit records and then to the exported report. With Exchange Manager, I tested participant and grant data across file imports and stock-plan applications. These projects taught me to check scheduling, transmission and content independently.

## Delta Dental · Enrollment, billing and payments

I led automation strategy and framework work for the data exchange platform. Tests followed partner enrollment data from EDI, XML or CSV transmission through parsing and downstream processing. On Oracle Revenue Management and Billing, I worked with developers to establish test data and verify how membership changes affected premiums and account balances.

One decision I would make earlier today is where to test billing rules. I initially invested in UI tests, but nested frames and dynamic controls made them costly while the critical logic lived in backend services. I made the case for service and database checks alongside focused UI coverage. I also worked with colleagues and an external consultant on shared automation standards and a Center of Excellence resource, matching frameworks to the applications and teams maintaining them.

## Wells Fargo · Digital banking offers

I tested offer presentation and disposition across public and signed-in banking journeys. Showing the right offer was one assertion; recording what the customer did was another. I followed interactions such as viewing, accepting and declining an offer into the underlying data so that a passing UI check could not hide a missing response.
