# 03 · Language Deep Dives

[Curriculum index](../README.md) · [Weekly planner](../planner.md)

**Study window** · 3–4

## What to study

Study generators and context managers in Python, promises and closures in JavaScript, narrowing and generics in TypeScript, and interfaces, streams and collections in Java.

## Practice assignment

Implement a reusable test-data builder in your primary language. Demonstrate that changing one case leaves the next case independent.
