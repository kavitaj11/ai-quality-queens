# 15 · Autonomous QA Pipelines

[Curriculum index](../README.md) · [Weekly planner](../planner.md)

**Study window** · 23–24

## What to study

Trace a detect–fix–learn loop through planning, execution and review. Study agent tests, MCP tools, audit records, LangGraph orchestration and cross-repository impact analysis.

## Practice assignment

Trace a simulated contract change from the producer to an affected consumer. Identify where the pipeline needs human review before applying a repair.
