## What to study

Map service boundaries, consumer contracts and dependencies. Use service virtualization with WireMock to make downstream behavior reproducible.

## Practice assignment

Model a dependency timeout. Specify the expected user outcome and the evidence that distinguishes a retry from a duplicate order.
