# 08 · Frameworks & BDD

[Curriculum index](../README.md) · [Weekly planner](../planner.md)

**Study window** · 13–15

## What to study

Compare pytest fixtures and parametrization with TestNG lifecycle methods and DataProviders. Connect Gherkin scenarios to step definitions and Allure reports. Explore Robot Framework and UFT as alternative approaches.

## Practice assignment

Express a shipping boundary as a business-readable scenario. Trace it from the feature file to the assertion and its report.
