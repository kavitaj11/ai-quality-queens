[Curriculum index](../README.md) · [Weekly planner](../planner.md)

**Study window** · 23

## What to study

Explore assisted test generation, flaky-test triage, self-healing locators, Healenium and visual testing platforms. Evaluate the evidence behind a proposed repair.

## Practice assignment

Review a locator substitution against the intended user action. Record a case where the substitute would silently test the wrong control.
