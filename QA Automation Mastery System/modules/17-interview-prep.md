# 17 · Interview Prep

[Curriculum index](../README.md) · [Weekly planner](../planner.md)

**Study window** · 24

## What to study

Practice coding, framework design and evidence-based QA stories. Apply them to a financial trading transfer, SQL validation, failure diagnosis, regression selection and handoff.

## Practice assignment

Present a transfer test strategy. Explain how you would investigate a duplicate posting and what evidence would justify a release decision.
