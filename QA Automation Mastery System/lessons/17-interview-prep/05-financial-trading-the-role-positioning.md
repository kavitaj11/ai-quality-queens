# Financial trading: the role & positioning

[17 · Module topics](../../modules/17-interview-prep.md) · [Curriculum index](../../README.md)

**Category** · DOMAIN SPECIFIC INTERVIEW PREP

What a large financial-services employer (e.g. Charles Schwab) is actually hiring for in a Senior QE Automation Engineer role, the question themes to expect, and the one-paragraph positioning statement to open with.

## Worked reference

````text
CHARLES SCHWAB — SENIOR QE AUTOMATION ENGINEER · INTERVIEW PREP

WHO THEY ARE ACTUALLY HIRING
  A hands-on Senior QA Automation Engineer / QE Lead — NOT a test
  architect who only produces strategy documents. They want someone
  who can walk in, assess the current environment, build a framework,
  automate APIs and regression testing, and leave behind something
  the team can maintain.

QUESTION THEMES TO EXPECT
  • Financial Services applications
  • High-volume transaction processing
  • Data integrity and accuracy
  • Regulatory / compliance concerns
  • API and microservices testing
  • CI/CD quality gates
  • Test automation ROI
  • Framework scalability

POSITION YOURSELF AS
  "I am a hands-on Quality Engineering leader with extensive
   experience building automation frameworks from scratch, driving
   API and backend testing strategies, and creating maintainable
   regression suites that integrate into CI/CD pipelines. My focus
   is not just creating automation, but building sustainable
   solutions that development and QA teams can own long after my
   engagement."

  That answer alone immediately aligns with the role.
````

## Exercise

Say the positioning statement out loud until it takes under 30 seconds and sounds like you, not a script.
