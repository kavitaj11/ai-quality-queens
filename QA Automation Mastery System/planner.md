## AI QA mastery

Continue at your own pace with Kavita Jadhav’s [Becoming an AI Quality Engineer](https://github.com/K11-Software-Solutions/becoming-ai-quality-engineer). The first eight modules follow the core roadmap. The last three deepen your evaluation practice.

- [ ] **AI 01** · [Transferable QA Skills](https://github.com/K11-Software-Solutions/becoming-ai-quality-engineer/blob/main/articles/01-transferable-qa-skills.md) · Map a familiar QA technique to an AI failure mode.
- [ ] **AI 02** · [Python Essentials for QA](https://github.com/K11-Software-Solutions/becoming-ai-quality-engineer/blob/main/articles/02-python-essentials-for-qa.md) · Write a parameterized pytest case using JSON test data.
- [ ] **AI 03** · [AI Fundamentals for Testers](https://github.com/K11-Software-Solutions/becoming-ai-quality-engineer/blob/main/articles/03-ai-fundamentals.md) · Trace a question through retrieval and answer generation.
- [ ] **AI 04** · [Evaluation Skills](https://github.com/K11-Software-Solutions/becoming-ai-quality-engineer/blob/main/articles/04-evaluation-skills.md) · Score a small set of answers for groundedness and correctness.
- [ ] **AI 05** · [Tools by Purpose](https://github.com/K11-Software-Solutions/becoming-ai-quality-engineer/blob/main/articles/05-tools-by-purpose.md) · Choose tools for one deterministic check and one evaluation.
- [ ] **AI 06** · [Roadmap and Hands-On Project](https://github.com/K11-Software-Solutions/becoming-ai-quality-engineer/blob/main/articles/06-roadmap-and-hands-on-project.md) · Run the offline RAG project and record its quality-gate results.
- [ ] **AI 07** · [Investigating Failures](https://github.com/K11-Software-Solutions/becoming-ai-quality-engineer/blob/main/articles/07-investigating-failures.md) · Trace a failed evaluation back to the stage that caused it.
- [ ] **AI 08** · [Portfolio and Interview Prep](https://github.com/K11-Software-Solutions/becoming-ai-quality-engineer/blob/main/articles/08-portfolio-and-interview-prep.md) · Write a portfolio case study explaining your release decision.
- [ ] **AI 09** · [Measuring Grader Severity](https://github.com/K11-Software-Solutions/becoming-ai-quality-engineer/blob/main/articles/09-measuring-grader-severity.md) · Compare grader severity across answer types.
- [ ] **AI 10** · [Using the Three Reports](https://github.com/K11-Software-Solutions/becoming-ai-quality-engineer/blob/main/articles/10-using-the-three-reports.md) · Use the three reports to explain one release risk.
- [ ] **AI 11** · [Pass/Fail or Graded?](https://github.com/K11-Software-Solutions/becoming-ai-quality-engineer/blob/main/articles/11-grading-completeness.md) · Compare binary and graded scoring on an incomplete answer.
